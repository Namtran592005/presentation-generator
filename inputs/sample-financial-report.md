# Q4 Financial Report Summary

## Revenue Growth
- Total revenue for Q4 reached $12.5M, a 23% increase YoY
- SaaS subscriptions contributed $8.2M (65% of total revenue)
- Professional services generated $3.1M
- Licensing revenue was $1.2M

## Operating Expenses
- R&D spending totaled $3.8M, representing 30% of revenue
- Sales & Marketing expenses were $2.9M
- G&A costs remained flat at $1.1M
- Total operating expenses: $7.8M

## Profitability Metrics
- Gross margin improved to 78% from 72% last year
- Operating income reached $4.7M (37.5% margin)
- Net income after tax: $3.5M
- EBITDA: $5.2M

## Key Growth Drivers
| Segment | Revenue | Growth |
|---------|---------|--------|
| Enterprise | 5.2 | 35 |
| Mid-Market | 3.8 | 28 |
| SMB | 2.1 | 15 |
| Public Sector | 1.4 | 10 |

## Outlook for Next Quarter
- Projected revenue guidance: $13.5M - $14.2M
- Planned expansion into APAC market
- New product launch scheduled for March
- Hiring 15 additional engineers
